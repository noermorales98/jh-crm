# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A small credit-repair agency (the owner plus 1–3 agents) serving mostly Spanish-speaking clients in the USA. Agents use the CRM during calls and follow-ups: they upload a client's credit report (MyFreeScoreNow PDF), read the analysis, prepare dispute rounds, quote and close the sale, and collect payments. Clients see some outputs indirectly: shared PDFs and copied plans.

## Product Purpose
JH CRM turns a raw three-bureau credit report into clear decisions: what is hurting the client, what to dispute, what to pay first, and how to sell the service honestly. Success is an agent who can explain a client's credit in a few minutes, create a correct dispute round without retyping data, and hand the client a plan they understand.

## Brand Personality
Clear, trustworthy, calm. It should feel like a serious financial tool (the restraint of Stripe or Mercury), not a sales gimmick. Spanish copy, plain words, no jargon without explanation. It speaks with expert confidence but never promises score points or deletions.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI templates: identical card grids, purple gradients, giant numbers without context, eyebrow labels on every section.
- A copy of Fondify. Fondify is the functional reference, but JH should look like its own product.
- "Get rich quick" credit apps: point promises, casino colors, fake urgency.

## Design Principles
- **Honest numbers.** Show what the report says and how it was computed; no score-point predictions, and flag estimates as estimates (CROA/FCRA).
- **One decision per screen.** Each panel answers one question (what to dispute, what to pay first) and ends in one primary action.
- **Bureau identity is consistent.** Equifax, Experian and TransUnion keep the same colors everywhere, on screen and in PDF.
- **Share-ready.** Anything an agent shows a client can be exported as a PDF that matches the screen.
- **Quiet by default.** Color is reserved for meaning (risk, bureau, status), not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in light and dark mode, never color alone for meaning (pair with text or icons), keyboard-operable controls, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation.
